## Scripture quotations

Scripture quotations are from the New Revised Standard Version (1989):

> New Revised Standard Version Bible, copyright © 1989 National Council of the Churches of Christ in the United States of America. Used by permission. All rights reserved worldwide.

The NRSV may be quoted without written permission under these conditions:

- up to 500 verses in total;
- less than an entire book of the Bible;
- less than 25 percent of the total words in the work;
- no changes to the text;
- the copyright notice above appears in the work.

Each paper carries the notice in its header, so a paper copied out of this repository keeps its notice.

The papers in this repository together quote no more than 500 distinct NRSV verses, so they remain within the limit even if the repository is treated as one work. The count is rechecked whenever a paper is added or revised. If the repository as a whole would pass 500 verses, each paper will be presented as a separate work, or permission will be sought from the National Council of the Churches of Christ in the USA.

Hebrew and Greek words cited from the original texts are not NRSV text and are not counted.

## Other quotations

- **The Canons of Dort** are quoted in a traditional English translation. The quotations are brief and attributed.
- **The Westminster Confession of Faith** (1646) is quoted in its original English, which is in the public domain.
- **Modern scholars** are quoted briefly, with full citations, for the facts of grammar, usage, or background the papers rely on.

## What this repository does not contain

This repository contains no NRSV text beyond the quotations in the papers themselves.
