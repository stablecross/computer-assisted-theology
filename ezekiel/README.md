# ezekiel

What Ezekiel's diagnosis of the heart and God's promised cure establish, and how far they reach.

## What this is

Ezekiel is sent to a people God says will not listen, and God says why: "they are not willing to listen to me; because all the house of Israel have a hard forehead and a stubborn heart" (Ezek 3:7).

The cure is God's act: "A new heart I will give you, and a new spirit I will put within you; and I will remove from your body the heart of stone and give you a heart of flesh" (36:26). Its stated result follows: "I will put my spirit within you, and make you follow my statutes" (36:27).

Those two sentences are narrated in Ezekiel. Two things are argued: that the heart of stone is the stubborn heart of 3:7, and that the call to repent is among the words that heart will not hear.

A reading that holds in one place must also hold everywhere else in Scripture. The criteria call this global propagation. So the paper does not stop at Ezekiel. It tests the diagnosis and the cure against the rest of the Old and New Testaments, and it asks how far they reach: from Ezekiel's audience to everyone who comes to Christ.
